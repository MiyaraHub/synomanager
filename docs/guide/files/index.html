Files

Your shares, from anywhere

File Station and Shared Folders, plus the three DSM apps that are really about content: Note Station, Synology Office and Contacts. If Download Station is installed it lives here too - it has its own page.

File Station

Browse and download

Starts at Root - every shared folder your account can see - and walks down from there. The breadcrumb at the top shows where you are and takes you back up.

  • Folders and files, each with the detail DSM holds: size, created and modified dates, owner and permissions.
  • Download any file. On Android and iOS the share sheet opens, so you can save it, open it elsewhere or send it on. On Windows and macOS you get a native Save As dialog.
  • It works away from home. Downloads go over whatever route the NAS is reachable on, QuickConnect relay included.
  • Upload a file to the NAS by picking it from your device.
What you can see is what DSM lets you see. File Station here respects your DSM account's permissions exactly - a folder your account cannot read does not appear, the same as in the web interface.
Shared Folders

The shares themselves

Not the files - the shares. Each entry shows its name, the volume it sits on, its description and an ACL chip when it carries access-control permissions.

  • Create a shared folder with the button at the bottom right.
  • The overflow on each row holds what you can do to that share.
  • Encrypted shares are identified as such, so you can tell at a glance which ones need unlocking after a reboot.
Where a share lives matters. The volume is shown on every row, which is the fastest way to answer "which volume is filling up" when you are looking at a full one on the Storage screen.
Note Station

Notebooks and notes

A summary at the top - total notes, notebooks and tags - then All Notes and each notebook with its own count.

  • Open any note and read its full content.
  • Create a notebook or add a note with the two buttons at the bottom right.
  • Delete a notebook from its row.

Three notebook titles in this screenshot are blurred - they are a real NAS's.

Synology Office

Installed, and what it supports

A status screen rather than an editor: the version of Synology Office your NAS runs, and which document types it supports - Documents, Spreadsheets and Slides - each with a tick.

Why it stops there. Office documents are stored in and opened through Synology Drive. Rather than build half a spreadsheet editor into a NAS monitor, the app tells you Office is there and points you at Drive, which is where the documents actually live.
Contacts

Address books

A total across every address book, then each book with its own count - including books imported from Google, which appear alongside the ones created on the NAS.

  • Search across everything from the box at the top.
  • Expand a book to see the contacts in it; the sections collapse so a book with two thousand entries does not swamp one with six.
  • Add a contact to a specific book from its row, or with the button at the bottom right.
  • Edit or delete an existing contact.

Common questions

Files FAQ

Can I download a file from my NAS to my phone?
Yes. Open File Station, find the file and download it. On mobile the share sheet takes over; on Windows and macOS you get a native Save As dialog. Downloads work over QuickConnect too.
Can I create a shared folder from the app?
Yes, from the Shared Folders sub-view. Existing shares show their name, their volume, their description and whether they carry ACL permissions.
Can I read my Note Station notes?
Yes. The Notes sub-view shows your notebooks with a note count and a tag count, and you can open any note to read it in full, create a notebook, or add a note.
Why does Synology Office only show a status?
Because Office documents are stored in and opened through Synology Drive. The app reports that Office is installed and which document types it supports; editing a document belongs in Synology's Drive app.
Why can I not see a folder I know exists?
File Station here respects your DSM account's permissions exactly. A folder your account cannot read does not appear, the same as in the web interface. Sign in as an account that can see it.
