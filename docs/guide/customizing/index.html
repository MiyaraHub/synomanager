Customizing

Four layers, all remembered

Cards, tabs, sub-views and collapse state. Every one of them can be reordered or switched off, every one is remembered between launches, and every one can be reset.

The map

Where each one lives

LayerWhereStored
Dashboard cardsThe pencil on the home screen, or Settings › Dashboard CategoriesPer NAS
Bottom navigation barSettings › Customize NavigationApp-wide
Sub-views inside a tabThe tab's ⋮ menu › Customize viewsPer NAS
Collapsed or expandedThe chevron on any card, with the default in Settings › Default Card ViewPer NAS
Help cardsDismiss on the card; restore in Settings › HelpApp-wide
Inside a tab

Customize views

Every tab with more than one section has a ⋮ menu at the top right, and Customize views in it.

  • A live preview of the sub-view row sits at the top, so you can see the result before saving.
  • A checkbox per view to show or hide it.
  • A drag handle to reorder - the first one in the list is the one the tab opens on.
  • At least one view must stay visible, so a tab cannot be made empty.
  • Reset restores the defaults; Save applies.
This is how you make a tab open where you want. If you always want the System tab to land on Logs, drag Logs to the top. The tab now opens there.
A design rule

Nothing you hide becomes a dead end

Hiding things creates a trap: a dashboard card whose screen you have hidden. Tap it, and where does it go?

It used to go to whatever that tab's first section happened to be - so hiding Disk Health and then tapping the Disk Health card landed you on Volumes & Pools, with no explanation. That reads as a broken app.

Now the card tells you. If the view is hidden it says so and offers a Customize button that takes you straight to the setting that would bring it back. If your NAS does not run the service at all, it says that instead - a different problem with a different answer, and the app does not confuse the two.

Starting over

Putting it all back

  • Dashboard: Reset in the edit toolbar restores the default cards and order.
  • Bottom bar: Reset in Customize Navigation.
  • Sub-views: Reset in each tab's Customize views.
  • Help cards: Restore all in Settings › Help, or restore them one at a time.
  • Everything, on a new phone: export and restore the app's settings.
New cards arrive switched on. When a version adds a card your NAS supports, it appears rather than waiting to be discovered in a settings screen. Customize is where you turn things off - not where you go to find out what exists.
Common questions

Customizing FAQ

What can I rearrange?
Which dashboard cards appear and in what order, which tabs are in the bottom bar and in what order, which sub-views appear inside each tab and in what order, and whether each card is expanded or collapsed. All four are remembered.
How do I hide a section inside a tab?
Open the tab, tap the three-dot menu at the top right and choose Customize views. Untick what you do not want and drag the rest into order. At least one view has to stay visible.
I hid something and now a card does nothing.
It tells you the view is hidden and offers a Customize button that takes you to the setting. It will not silently open a different screen.
Is my layout stored on the NAS or on the phone?
On the phone, per NAS. To move it to a new phone, use Backup and Restore.
