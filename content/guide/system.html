System Health

The readings

CardWhat it shows
CPU & RAM gaugesTotal CPU as a ring, with the user share called out underneath so you can tell your own workload from system overhead. RAM shows the percentage and the absolute figures.
MemoryUsed against total, as a bar. Green until it matters.

Cooling

Fan speed mode, changed from your phone

The Cooling card shows fan status and the mode the NAS is in, as a three-way control:

  • Full Speed - maximum airflow, maximum noise. Right for a rack or a cupboard.
  • Cool - the balanced default.
  • Quiet - lower speed, higher temperatures. Right for a NAS in a living room, if the drives can take it.
Confirmed, not assumed. The app reads the current mode from the NAS, writes your choice, then reads it back. A mode the NAS quietly refused is reported as refused, rather than sitting on screen looking as though it applied.
An unrecognised mode stays read-only. If your NAS reports a cooling mode outside those three, the card shows what it reported and does not offer to change it - rather than mislabelling it as one of the three it knows.
Identify & Alerts

Find the box, silence the buzzer

  • Identify blinks the NAS's status light so you can tell which unit you are standing in front of. Stop ends it.
  • Silence beep stops the alarm buzzer. It silences the noise, not the condition - whatever set the alarm off is still true, and the health header will still say so.
DSM Update

Is there a firmware update

The DSM Update card reports the version you are on and whether Synology has something newer. Check for update asks the NAS to look again.

This is your NAS's firmware, not the app. Syno Manager updates itself through the App Store or Google Play like any other app. This card is DSM's own update surface, the same one in Control Panel.
Power Controls

Restart and shut down

At the bottom of System Health, and marked in red, because they affect every connected user - including the person mid-way through a file copy.

  • Restart NAS - a clean reboot. Confirmed before it runs.
  • Shutdown NAS - a clean power-off. Confirmed before it runs.
Think before shutting down remotely. A powered-off NAS cannot relay its own wake packet. Bringing it back from outside your network needs a VPN into it, or a router set up to forward Wake-on-LAN. See Wake-on-LAN for what works from where.
Shortcuts do not skip the confirmation. The synomanager://reboot and synomanager://shutdown links deliberately only open the app at this screen. A destructive action does not become one-tap because it was triggered from a launcher.
Resources

Live charts

The Resources sub-view is the same data as a rolling graph, which is what you want when the question is "is this a spike or is this constant?"

  • CPU Usage with the user and system split.
  • Memory Usage with used against total.
  • Disk Read and Disk Write, each with its own chart, plus overall disk utilisation.
  • Load Averages over one, five and fifteen minutes.
Charts fill at the polling interval. A shorter interval gives finer detail and costs more battery; a longer one is kinder and coarser.
Logs

What DSM recorded

Two tabs: System for DSM's own log, and Connection for sign-ins and file access. Both are read when you ask for them.

  • It loads once and holds still. A log that re-reads on a timer while you are trying to read a line is useless, and it costs battery and network for nothing.
  • Press play to follow live. Press it again to pause. Following stops on its own when you leave the screen or send the app to the background, so a forgotten log tab is not quietly polling in your pocket.
  • There is a search box for finding one line in a long log.
  • Container and stack logs work the same way - see Docker.
If the log will not load and reports a connection timeout while the rest of the app is fine, the Log Center service on the NAS is the thing not answering, not your connection. Check that Log Center is installed and running in DSM's Package Center - the system log API is part of it.
Common questions

System FAQ

Can I change the fan speed from the app?
Yes. System Health has a Cooling card with Full Speed, Cool and Quiet. The app reads the mode the NAS is currently in, writes your choice, and reads it back to confirm. A NAS reporting a mode outside those three keeps a read-only display rather than being misrepresented.
Can I restart or shut down my NAS from my phone?
Yes, from Power Controls at the bottom of System Health. Both are confirmed before they run. Shutting down remotely is worth thinking twice about - a powered-off NAS cannot be woken over the internet without a VPN or a router configured for Wake-on-LAN.
What is Identify NAS for?
It blinks the NAS's status light so you can tell which box in a rack or a cupboard you are looking at. Silence beep stops the alarm buzzer without silencing the condition that set it off.
Do the logs refresh by themselves?
No, and deliberately. A log loads once and holds still so you can read it. Press play to follow it live, press again to pause, and it stops following on its own when you leave the screen or background the app.
Why is my load average high when CPU looks fine?
Load average counts processes waiting, and they are often waiting on disks rather than CPU. Check Disk I/O utilisation on the same screen - a high utilisation percentage with low CPU is the usual explanation.
