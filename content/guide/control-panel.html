Users

DSM accounts

Every account on the NAS, with its display name, its description and its email address where one is set. An Expired chip marks accounts DSM has disabled.

What you can do

  • Add a user with the details DSM asks for.
  • Edit an existing account.
  • Change a password.
  • Delete an account you created.
System accounts cannot be deleted. admin, guest and the service accounts DSM creates for its own packages are detected and protected. Removing one of those breaks the NAS in ways that are tedious to undo, and a phone is not the place to find that out.

Task Scheduler

What is scheduled, and what runs next

Every task DSM has, whether it is enabled, what it does and when it next fires.

  • The task name and its type - a Hyper Backup task, a user-defined script, a recycle-bin clean-up - each labelled with what kind of thing it is.
  • What it actually does. A script task shows the command it runs; a backup task shows its description.
  • Next run, as a date and time.
  • A switch per task to enable or disable it without opening DSM.
  • Run Now on tasks DSM allows to be triggered on demand.
Run Now runs it now. A scheduled script that expects to run at 3am on an idle NAS will behave differently at 6pm during a file copy. The button does what it says.
Power

The UPS

Present when a UPS is attached. One card, everything on it.

ShownMeaning
Manufacturer and modelAs the UPS reports itself.
StatusOnline on mains, or on battery.
ChargePercentage and a bar. Amber below 50%, red below 20%.
ModeHow the NAS is talking to it - USB, or over the network.
RuntimeHow long the UPS estimates it can hold the load.
EnabledWhether UPS support is switched on in DSM at all.
Charge feeds the health badge. A UPS that is discharging shows up on the home screen without you opening this tab - which is the point, because by the time you think to check, you may not have long.

Common questions

Control Panel FAQ

Can I add or edit DSM users from the app?
Yes. Add a user, edit their details, change a password or delete an account. System accounts - admin, guest and the service accounts DSM creates - are detected and protected from deletion.
Can I run a scheduled task manually?
Yes, for tasks DSM allows to be triggered. You can also enable or disable any task without opening DSM.
What does the UPS screen show?
The UPS model, whether it is online or on battery, its charge as a percentage and a bar, how it is connected, the estimated runtime left, and whether UPS support is enabled on the NAS.
Can I change the shutdown-on-battery policy?
No. The app reports UPS state; the policy is set in DSM under Hardware and Power, where the consequences are spelled out.
Why is the Power sub-view missing?
Because no UPS is attached, or UPS support is switched off in DSM. The app hides screens for hardware that is not there.
