Shortcuts & links

Straight to the screen you want

Syno Manager registers the synomanager:// URL scheme, so a Siri phrase, a launcher shortcut or an automation app can open the app exactly where you need it instead of on the home screen.

The links

Every shortcut

LinkOpens
synomanager://dashboardThe home screen.
synomanager://storageStorage - volumes, disk health, external.
synomanager://systemSystem - health, resources, logs.
synomanager://networkNetwork - throughput, connections, services, VPN, DDNS.
synomanager://appsApps - packages, Docker, projects, virtual machines.
synomanager://backupBackup - Hyper Backup and Active Backup.
synomanager://filesFiles - File Station and shares.
synomanager://wakeThe Wake devices screen.
synomanager://rebootThe power controls. Opens only - see below.
synomanager://shutdownThe power controls. Opens only - see below.
A deliberate limit

Why reboot and shutdown only open the app

They take you to the power controls. They do not restart or shut down anything on their own.

A shortcut is a thing you can hit by accident - a mis-tapped launcher icon, a misheard voice phrase, an automation that fires on the wrong trigger. Restarting a NAS disconnects everyone using it and interrupts whatever it was doing. Those two facts do not belong together, so the link stops at the screen and the existing confirmation still applies.

Every other link is safe by nature, because all it does is show you something. That is why the rest are one tap and these two are not.
Using them

On each platform

iOS - Siri and Shortcuts

  1. Open Shortcuts and create a new shortcut.
  2. Add the Open URLs action.
  3. Enter the link, for example synomanager://storage.
  4. Name it something you would say out loud - "Check my NAS storage".
  5. Add it to the home screen, or just ask Siri.

Android - launchers and automation

  1. Most launchers can place a shortcut that opens a URL.
  2. Automation apps - Tasker, MacroDroid and the like - can open one from any trigger.
  3. An <a href="synomanager://system"> link in a note or a page works too.
Which NAS it opens on follows your Launch Behavior setting. A shortcut chooses a screen, not a device - so with "Last NAS I used" set, synomanager://storage shows the storage of whichever NAS you were last on.
Common questions

Shortcuts FAQ

Does Syno Manager have a URL scheme?
Yes. synomanager://<name> opens the app on a specific screen. Dashboard, storage, system, network, apps, backup, files and wake each have one.
Can a shortcut restart my NAS in one tap?
No, and deliberately. The reboot and shutdown links only open the app at the power controls, where the existing confirmation applies.
Can I use these with Siri?
Yes. Create a shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app that opens the URL, give it a phrase, and Siri will take you straight there.
Do shortcuts pick a specific NAS?
No. A shortcut opens a screen; which NAS it opens on follows your Launch Behavior setting.
