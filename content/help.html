When something is not right

Most problems fall into one of a handful of shapes. Each one below says what the symptom actually means, which is usually the fastest route to the fix.

Jump to
  • Cannot connect
  • Sign-in refused
  • Missing tabs or cards
  • Blank photo tiles
  • Cameras away from home
  • Logs will not load
  • Stale widgets
  • No Assistant tab
  • Still stuck
Connection

"Connection timeout"

This means nothing answered. Your credentials were never tried, so the problem is the address, the port, or the network - never the password.

  1. Open DSM in the phone's browser at exactly the address and port you typed. If the browser cannot reach it either, the app is not the problem.
  2. Check HTTPS matches the port. 5001 is the HTTPS listener and 5000 is the HTTP one. Crossing them times out.
  3. Check the phone's network. A local address like 192.168.1.20 does not exist on mobile data. Join the Wi-Fi, or use QuickConnect, a domain or a VPN.
  4. On iOS, allow local network access. Settings › Apps › Syno Manager › Local Network. Without it iOS silently blocks connections to your own network, and the symptom is a timeout that looks like a network fault.
  5. Check DSM's firewall. Control Panel › Security › Firewall - a rule allowing only certain addresses will block a phone that is not one of them.
  6. Check the NAS is awake. A sleeping NAS does not answer. Wake it first.

Missing things

A tab, sub-view or card is not there

There are exactly three reasons, and they have different fixes.

  1. The package is not installed. The app reads what your NAS can do and hides everything it cannot. No Download Station package, no Download Station tab. Install it from Package Center.
  2. It was installed after you connected. The capability list is read on connect. Pull to refresh, or reopen the app, and it appears.
  3. It is hidden. Check the tab's ⋮ menu › Customize views, and Settings › Dashboard Categories for cards. See customizing.
Tapping a card whose view is hidden will tell you so and offer a Customize button. If it says your NAS does not offer that view, the package genuinely is not there.
Photos

Blank tiles in the photo library

  • Recent iPhone photos: these are HEIC, and on DSM 7.2.2 and later the NAS no longer builds previews for them. Syno Manager decodes them on the device instead, so they should appear. If they do not, make sure you are on a current version of the app.
  • Older photos blank too: the NAS's thumbnails are genuinely missing. Use re-index on the Photos screen and let the NAS rebuild them. It takes a while on a large library and runs on the NAS, so you can close the app.
  • Everything blank and slow over QuickConnect: the relay is rate-limited. Give it longer, or use a direct entry when you are at home.
Cameras

Live view works at home but not away

One setting fixes this: Tunnel camera live view through DSM port, in the NAS's configuration.

  • Off - live view connects straight to RTSP on port 554. Fast on your own network, but that port is almost never reachable from outside.
  • On - the stream is tunnelled over the DSM HTTPS port you already connect on. Nothing extra to expose, and it works from anywhere you can reach DSM.
  • A camera showing "Tap to view" is not broken. It is H.265, and DSM does not produce still snapshots for those. Tap it and the stream opens.
Logs

The system log will not load

If the log reports a connection timeout while the rest of the app works fine, the thing not answering is the Log Center service on the NAS, not your connection.

  • Check Log Center is installed and running in DSM's Package Center. The system log API belongs to it.
  • Check the account's permissions. Reading system logs needs administrator rights on most DSM configurations.
  • Container and stack logs are separate and come from Docker, so those can work while the system log does not.
Widgets

The home screen widget is out of date

Android and iOS decide when a widget may refresh, and both ration it hard to save battery. No app can force one - which is why each widget prints the time its numbers were taken instead of a promise.

  • Open the app - the surest refresh.
  • Android: exclude Syno Manager from battery optimisation.
  • iOS: make sure Background App Refresh is on for the app.
  • Check reachability: a widget cannot read a NAS the phone cannot reach. On mobile data with a local-IP-only entry, it will not update until you are home.
  • "never updated" means no successful reading yet - freshly added, or the app has not run since.
Assistant

There is no Assistant tab

The assistant needs an on-device model, and the tab is hidden entirely on hardware that does not have one - a control that cannot work is worse than no control.

  • Android needs AICore with a Gemini Nano model available to it.
  • iOS and macOS need Apple Intelligence supported and enabled.
  • Check what your device reports in Settings › On-Device AI, which names the state it found.
Still stuck

Report it

Bugs and feature requests go in the public tracker, where they get a number and a thread rather than disappearing into an inbox.

Report a bug or request a feature

What to include: the app version (Settings shows it inline, and About has the build number), your DSM version, your NAS model, whether you are on your own network or away, and what you expected instead. A screenshot with anything private cropped out helps more than a description.
Common questions

Troubleshooting FAQ

It says connection timeout. What does that mean?
That nothing answered at the address and port you gave - the credentials were never tried. Check that HTTPS matches the port, that the phone is on a network that can reach the NAS, and that DSM's own web interface loads in the phone's browser at the same address.
Why does it say invalid credentials when my password is right?
Because the NAS answered and refused the sign-in. Check for a typo, check whether the account uses 2FA, and check DSM's auto-block list in case repeated attempts have blocked your phone's address.
Why can I not see a tab I expect?
Either the package behind it is not installed, or the sub-view is hidden. A package installed since you connected needs a refresh - pull down, or reopen the app.
My cameras work at home but not away.
Turn on Tunnel camera live view through DSM port. With it off, live view wants RTSP on port 554, which is usually not reachable from outside.
My photos show as blank tiles.
HEIC photos are decoded on the device because DSM 7.2.2+ no longer builds previews for them. If older photos are blank too, use re-index on the Photos screen.
Why is my widget showing old numbers?
Because the operating system decides when a widget may refresh. Each widget shows when its data was captured for exactly that reason; opening the app is the surest refresh.
